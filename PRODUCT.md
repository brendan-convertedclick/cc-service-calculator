# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The internal Converted Click team — the agency's own operators, working in Conductor daily and weekly to run the business. They are power users: fluent in the tool, returning to the same surfaces constantly, and better served by speed and density than by hand-holding or first-run tours. There is a single shared login (no per-user roles in V1), so the interface assumes a small, known, expert audience rather than a public one. A subset of what the app produces — SoWs, quotes, invoice line items — can end up in front of clients, so those output surfaces carry a slightly higher polish bar than the internal working screens.

## Product Purpose

Conductor is the agency's operations command center: the single place Converted Click is run from day to day. It pulls the whole pipeline into one system — inbound briefs, scoping, projects, retainers, team briefing, productivity, estimation, and reconciliation — replacing the scattered mix of spreadsheets, ClickUp, and Xero glue that the work otherwise lives across. Success is when an operator can move from a client request to scoped work, team briefs, and invoice-ready numbers without leaving the app or losing trust in the figures along the way.

## Positioning

One operational hub for running the agency — briefs, projects, retainers, productivity, and reconciliation in a single trustworthy surface, instead of stitched-together spreadsheets and disconnected tools.

## Brand Personality

Warm and approachable, but unmistakably capable. The voice is human and friendly — plain, considerate copy over terse system-speak — without ever sacrificing the it-just-works confidence the team relies on. The feeling to aim for is a calm, competent colleague: easy to work alongside, never cold or bureaucratic, and never casual to the point of undermining trust in money, margins, and estimates. Warmth lives in the copy, the empty states, and the small moments of feedback; precision lives in the numbers and the flows.

## Anti-references

Not enterprise/SAP heaviness — no cluttered, gray, joyless legacy-admin feel where every screen is a form. Not the generic AI-SaaS template — no purple-gradient hero, identical card grids, tiny tracked eyebrows, or big-number-metric clichés that make a dashboard look like every other dashboard. And explicitly not spreadsheet sprawl: Conductor exists to replace scattered raw grids, so recreating endless hierarchy-less tables inside the app is a failure, not a feature.

## Design Principles

The tool disappears into the task. Earned familiarity beats novelty — standard affordances, consistent component vocabulary screen to screen, no invented controls for ordinary jobs. A power user should never pause at a subtly-off element.

Warm competence. Be the calm, capable colleague: human copy and considered feedback, never SAP-cold and never cute-gamified. Warmth is carried by tone and moments, not by decoration on every surface.

Replace the spreadsheet, don't rebuild it. Bring hierarchy, structure, and meaning to dense data — group, summarize, and lead the eye — rather than surfacing raw grids the operator has to decode.

Money earns precision. This app scopes work, margins, and invoices; numbers and money-bearing flows must read as trustworthy and exact, with client-facing outputs (SoWs, quotes, invoices) held to a visibly higher finish.

Density with calm. Pack real signal for daily operators without clutter — respect that these are expert users who want speed, while keeping the surface legible and unhurried.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA with sensible defaults: AA contrast on text and controls, visible focus states, full keyboard navigability, and `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation. No formal audit or AAA target for V1, but the baseline should hold across the app so daily keyboard-driven use stays comfortable.
